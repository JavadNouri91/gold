Q3.2 → Other

Gold credit is a Rial-denominated credit limit assigned by the
seller to the customer.

It is NOT physical gold inventory and NOT a pre-purchased gold balance.

The customer's gold trading capacity is calculated dynamically
based on the Rial credit limit and the current applicable gold price.

Example:
Rial Gold Credit = 1,000,000,000 Toman
Current applicable gold price = 10,000,000 Toman/gram
Available capacity ≈ 100 grams.

Q3.3 → Credit is reserved at Order submission.
=========================================================================
When the customer submits an Order, the required Rial-based credit
is immediately blocked/reserved.

The reserved amount remains blocked while the Order is awaiting
manual review.

If the Order is:
- Approved → reserved credit becomes consumed.
- Rejected → reserved credit is automatically released.
- Cancelled by customer → reserved credit is automatically released.
- Cancelled by authorized staff → reserved credit is automatically released.
====================================================================
Q3.4 → Hard Block

If the order exceeds the customer's available Rial-based credit
limit, the system must immediately reject/block the order.

The customer cannot submit the order for manual review or
manager override.

Only orders within the customer's available credit limit can
proceed to quotation and manual trade approval.

======================**********************************************^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^===========================
Customer Cancellation:
Configurable by system settings.

The system must provide a setting that determines whether a customer
is allowed to cancel an Order before manual approval.

If enabled:
Customer can cancel before Trade approval.

If disabled:
Customer cannot cancel after Order submission; cancellation must be
performed by an authorized Operator/Manager according to permissions.
================================

Q3.5 → Credit is released immediately and automatically.

When an Order is rejected or cancelled, the reserved/blocked credit
must be released immediately and automatically.

This applies to:
- Customer cancellation
- Operator/Reviewer/Manager cancellation
- Order rejection

If the Trade is approved, the reserved credit becomes consumed.

IMPORTANT:

"Gold Credit" must NOT be modeled as physical gold inventory.

It represents a gold-denominated customer credit/obligation.

The accounting system must support both:
1. Financial Ledger
2. Gold Ledger

Ledger entries must be traceable to their source transaction and must not be physically deleted. Corrections must use reversal/adjustment entries.
